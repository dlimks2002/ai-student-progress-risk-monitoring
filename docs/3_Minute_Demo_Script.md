# 3-Minute Video Demo Script

## 0:00–0:20 — Introduction
“Hi, this is my prototype for **AI-Powered Student Progress & Risk Monitoring**.

The objective is to help lecturers identify students who may require early academic support before their performance deteriorates further.

The prototype uses four indicators: attendance, assessment performance, engagement and assignment submission.”

## 0:20–0:55 — Dashboard Overview
“On the main dashboard, lecturers can immediately see the number of students being monitored, how many are high or medium risk, and the average risk score.

The risk distribution chart provides a quick overview of the class.

Lecturers can also filter students by risk level or search for a specific student.”

## 0:55–1:35 — Risk Identification
“I’ll now filter the dashboard to high-risk students.

For example, Isaac Goh has been identified as high risk.

His risk score is driven by several indicators: relatively low attendance, weak assessment performance, low engagement and incomplete assignment submission.

This is important because the system does not simply label the student as high risk — it also explains the factors contributing to the flag.”

## 1:35–2:15 — Student Drill-Down
“When I select the student, I can see the individual indicators and the reasons for the risk flag.

For example, if attendance is below the monitoring threshold or assessment performance is low, the dashboard highlights those specific areas.

This makes the risk score more transparent and allows the lecturer to understand what may need attention.”

## 2:15–2:40 — Intervention Recommendation
“The prototype also provides suggested intervention actions.

For this student, the lecturer could arrange a consultation, identify barriers affecting attendance, recommend targeted revision and create a catch-up plan for missed assignments.

The intention is not to automatically make decisions about students, but to help lecturers prioritise early support.”

## 2:40–3:00 — Conclusion
“In summary, this prototype demonstrates an end-to-end workflow: student data is processed, an explainable risk score is generated, students are categorised by risk level, contributing factors are displayed, and possible interventions are suggested.

The current version uses synthetic data, but the architecture can be extended to real LMS data, machine learning models and intervention tracking in a future implementation.

Thank you.”
